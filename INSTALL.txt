FabStudio – Virtual Try-On (test build 0.1.0)
===========================================

Try clothes on yourself with your webcam. Everything runs on your computer;
no video or photos are uploaded.

INSTALL (Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser)
1. Unzip this file to a folder you'll keep (e.g. Documents\FabStudio).
   Don't delete the folder afterwards: the browser loads the extension from it.
2. Open chrome://extensions  (Edge: edge://extensions)
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder: the one that
   contains manifest.json.
5. Optional: pin FabStudio from the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar.

Chrome will say the extension can "read and change all your data on all
websites". That's only so it can download the product photos you pick.

USE
1. Click the FabStudio icon. A side panel opens.
2. Click "Start camera". A tab asks for camera access: click Allow
   (choose "Allow on every visit" if offered). The tab closes and the
   camera starts in the panel. The first start downloads the body-tracking
   models (~40 MB) once.
3. Add a garment:
   - on a shopping site, right-click a product photo -> "Try on with FabStudio", or
   - Garments tab -> "+ Upload garment".
   Best: a model standing straight facing the camera, or the top alone on a
   plain background, or a transparent PNG. Tops only (t-shirts, shirts,
   hoodies, jackets).
4. Stand 1.5-2.5 m back so your head, shoulders and hips are in view.
   "Demo" shows a test mannequin without a camera.

KNOWN LIMITS
- It's a 2D photo warped onto you: no back side, no real 3D draping.
- The toolbar icon is a grey placeholder "A".
- Warnings like "INFO: Created TensorFlow Lite XNNPACK delegate" under
  Errors on the extensions page are normal log messages.

FEEDBACK
If something goes wrong, send a screenshot of the panel and, if possible,
right-click the panel -> Inspect -> Console, and copy any red lines.
